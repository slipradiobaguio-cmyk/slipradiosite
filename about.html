slip radio is a community web radio streaming live from baguio city.
slip radio is a space to gather, listen, experiment, and play; an invitation to collide and collaborate through music and sound.
slip radio foregrounds local artists, selectors, amateur broadcasters, and cultural workers.

we aim to support and promote baguio's cultural scene, and explore local sonic practices and expressions beyond existing events and venues.
we value capacity-building and knowledge exchange by providing our supporter-community and volunteers access to our space and facilities. we value mutual care, trust, and respect in running our community platform.
