SUPPORT US

As an emerging community platform, slip radio is committed to foregrounding local voices, regional sonic cultures, and amateur broadcasters. We are a non-profit, non-commercial radio, currently operating mostly out of our team's pockets (and passion!).

Thanks to the community's support, we can continue our weekly broadcasts and maintain our studio to hold space for our local DJs and hosts to practice and grow.
